# Mailtrix

  AI-powered email assistant for Microsoft Outlook Desktop.

  ## Download

  Get the latest installer at **[mailtrix.pro/download](https://mailtrix.pro/download)**

  ## About

  Mailtrix connects directly to your Outlook Desktop Classic via local COM automation — no OAuth, no cloud intermediary. Your email data stays on your machine; only conversation prompts are sent to your chosen AI provider.

  **Features:**
  - Natural language email management (read, search, send, organize)
  - Multiple AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini)
  - Bring Your Own Key or use the managed plan
  - Privacy-first: local-only email access

  **Requirements:**
  - Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
  - Microsoft Outlook Desktop Classic (OUTLOOK.EXE)
